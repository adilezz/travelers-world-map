**The bevel.** Doc 3 §7.1 requires bevelled corners on the tiles. MapLibre
cannot do it at any version. Accept square edges, fake a chamfer with a second
inset extrusion, or write a custom WebGL layer — the owner's call, already
raised with him. Build none of the three until the answer comes back, and do
not ship square edges as though the requirement were met.
